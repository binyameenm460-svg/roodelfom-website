Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Roodelfom ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our backend API service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create a Roodelfom account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Username
  • Password (encrypted)
  • Account creation date

1.2 Payment Information

When you subscribe to our $29/month plan, we collect:

  • Payment method (processed by Paddle/Safepay, not stored by us)
  • Billing address
  • Transaction history
  • Subscription status

1.3 API Usage Data

We automatically collect when you use our API:

  • API requests and responses
  • API endpoints accessed
  • Timestamps of API calls
  • Your API key usage
  • Error logs and debugging information
  • Response times and performance metrics

1.4 Technical Data

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Cookies and tracking identifiers

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:

  • Service Delivery - Providing API access and managing your account
  • Billing - Processing payments and managing subscriptions
  • Support - Responding to customer inquiries and technical support
  • Analytics - Understanding usage patterns and improving our service
  • Security - Detecting and preventing fraud and security issues
  • Communications - Sending service updates and important notices
  • Legal Compliance - Meeting regulatory and legal requirements

3. Data Storage & Security

3.1 Where We Store Data

Your data is stored on:

  • PostgreSQL database on Railway (cloud infrastructure)
  • Geographically distributed servers for redundancy
  • Encrypted data at rest

3.2 Security Measures

We protect your data using:

  • SSL/TLS encryption (HTTPS)
  • Password hashing (bcryptjs)
  • API key authentication
  • Regular security monitoring
  • Access controls and firewalls

4. Third-Party Services

We share limited information with:

4.1 Payment Processors

Paddle or Safepay processes payments. They receive:

  • Email address
  • Billing information
  • Payment method details

Review their privacy policies: Paddle.com | Safepay.com.pk

4.2 Cloud Infrastructure

Railway hosts our infrastructure. They access:

  • Database backups
  • Server logs
  • System monitoring data

Review their privacy policy: Railway.app

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for:

  • Active Accounts - Duration of subscription + 30 days after cancellation
  • API Logs - 90 days for security and debugging
  • Payment Records - 7 years (legal/tax compliance)
  • Deleted Accounts - Permanently deleted, except where legally required

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access - Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction - Update or correct your information
  • Deletion - Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export - Download your data in standard format
  • Opt-out - Unsubscribe from marketing communications

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@roodelfom.com

7. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies for:

  • Authentication (session management)
  • User preferences
  • Analytics (understanding usage)

Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies. Blocking cookies may affect service functionality.

8. Children's Privacy

Roodelfom is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover we've collected data from a child, we'll delete it immediately.

9. International Users

If you're outside Pakistan, your data may be transferred internationally. By using Roodelfom, you consent to this transfer under our data protection standards.

10. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of Roodelfom after changes means you accept the new policy.
