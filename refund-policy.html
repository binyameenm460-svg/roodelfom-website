Refund Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Simple Refund Policy: We want you to be satisfied. If you're not happy with Roodelfom within 14 days of your first charge, we'll refund you. No questions asked.

1. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you subscribe to Roodelfom's Pro Plan ($29/month) and are not completely satisfied, you can request a full refund within 14 days of your initial charge.

How to Request a Refund:

  • Email: support@roodelfom.com
  • Subject: "Refund Request - [Your Email]"
  • Include your account email and reason (optional)

Processing Time: Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days after approval.

2. Refund Eligibility

You ARE Eligible for a Refund If:

  • You request it within 14 days of your first charge
  • You have an active or recently cancelled Roodelfom account
  • You haven't violated our Terms of Service
  • Your refund request is genuine and in good faith

You ARE NOT Eligible If:

  • 14+ days have passed since your initial charge
  • You're requesting a refund for a second/third/subsequent month (see section 3)
  • You violated our Terms of Service (abuse, illegal activity, etc.)
  • You're filing a chargeback or dispute (see section 4)
  • Your refund request is fraudulent or in bad faith

3. Recurring Billing & Cancellation

How Cancellation Works:

Your Roodelfom subscription automatically renews monthly. When you cancel:

  • Cancellation takes effect immediately
  • No charge in the next billing cycle
  • You keep API access until your current billing period ends
  • No refund for the current month (you've already used the service)

To Cancel Your Subscription:

Email: billing@roodelfom.com with subject "Cancel Subscription"

4. Refund for Subsequent Months (After 14 Days)

Important: Refunds are only available for your first month within 14 days. Subsequent charges are non-refundable but can be cancelled to stop future charges.

If you've been charged for a second, third, or later month:

  • No refund available - You've had access to the service
  • Can cancel anytime - No charge for the next month
  • Pro tip: Cancel immediately after your first month if unsure

5. Refund Processing & Payment Methods

Refund Timeline:

  • Request received → Reviewed within 2 business days
  • Approved → Refund issued to original payment method
  • Processing time → 5-10 business days (depends on your bank)

Payment Method Refunds:

Refunds are issued to your original payment method:

  • Credit/Debit Card: Appears as a credit on your statement within 5-10 business days
  • Bank Transfer: Returned to your bank account within 5-10 business days
  • Paddle/Safepay: Processed through your payment processor

6. Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

DO NOT file a chargeback. Contact us first before disputing a charge.

If You Have a Payment Issue:

  1. Email us FIRST: billing@roodelfom.com
  2. Describe the problem
  3. We'll resolve it within 2 business days
  4. If unsatisfied, THEN dispute with your bank

Chargeback Policy:

If you file a chargeback without contacting us first:

  • Your account will be terminated immediately
  • API access will be revoked
  • You'll be banned from creating new accounts
  • You'll be liable for chargeback fees ($15-$100)

Chargebacks should be a LAST RESORT, not a first step.

7. Special Circumstances

Service Outages or Issues:

If Roodelfom experiences significant downtime or issues:

  • We'll offer a credit for the affected period
  • Or a partial refund if requested
  • Contact: support@roodelfom.com

Billing Errors:

If you were charged incorrectly:

  • Double charges - Full refund of duplicate
  • Wrong amount - Refund or credit for the difference
  • Unauthorized charge - Full refund + account review

8. Refund Disputes & Appeals

If your refund is denied:

  • You'll receive an explanation via email
  • You may appeal within 7 days
  • Include additional information supporting your request
  • Our team will review and respond within 2 business days

9. Tax Implications

Refunds are issued minus applicable taxes (if any). You're responsible for understanding the tax implications of refunds in your jurisdiction.

10. Contact Information

Questions about refunds? Contact us:

  • Billing Questions: billing@roodelfom.com
  • Refund Requests: support@roodelfom.com
  • Website: roodelfom.com

Include your account email, subscription date, and reason for refund request.

11. Policy Changes

Roodelfom may update this Refund Policy at any time. Changes take effect immediately. Your continued use means you accept the updated policy.
